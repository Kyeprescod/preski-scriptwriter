Privacy policy

Last updated: October 2026. This is a plain-English template. Have it checked before launch.

Who I am

Preski Fitness is run by Kye Prescod in the United Kingdom. If you have any questions about your data, email hello@preskifitness.com.

What I collect

  • Your name and email when you download a free guide.
  • The answers you give in the coaching application.
  • Payment details when you buy the programme. These are handled by Stripe; I never see your card number.
  • Messages you type into the chat assistant on this site.

How I use it

  • To send you the guide you asked for, plus training tips and offers by email (you can unsubscribe in one click from any email).
  • To review your coaching application and get back to you.
  • To deliver products you've bought.
  • To answer your questions in the chat.

Who I share it with

Only the services that run this site: Resend (email delivery), Netlify (website hosting), Stripe (payments) and Anthropic (the AI that powers the chat assistant). I never sell your data.

How long I keep it

Until you unsubscribe or ask me to delete it. Purchase records are kept as long as UK tax law requires.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR you can ask to see, correct or delete your data at any time. Email me and I'll sort it within 30 days. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).
