Questions

Before you ask

Do I need any equipment?

No. A pair of dumbbells helps, but every session has a bodyweight version. A backpack filled with books works as extra weight.

I'm a complete beginner. Is this for me?

Yes. Every exercise has an easier version, and we build up week by week. Most of my audience started from zero.

What's the difference between coaching and the programme?

Coaching is built around you, with weekly check-ins, nutrition targets and form reviews. The programme is a fixed 8-week plan you follow yourself. Same training style, different level of support.

Will I have to give up foods I love?

No. You'll hit a protein target and a small calorie deficit. Your favourite foods fit inside that. Banning food is why most diets fail.

How much time do I need?

Four sessions a week at 25 to 45 minutes each, plus daily walking. It's built for people with jobs and families.

How do check-ins work?

Once a week you send your weight, progress photos and a few quick answers. I review them and update your plan within 48 hours.
