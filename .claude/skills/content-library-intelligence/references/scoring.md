# Scoring rubric (1-10, whole or half points)

Scores are judgements from the contact sheet. If it can't be judged, leave the field out (null). Don't default everything to 7.

## visual_quality_score (how it looks)
Average, mentally, of: sharpness, lighting, composition, framing, subject visibility, professional appearance. Weight stability separately in `stability_score`.
- 9-10 crisp, well lit, subject framed deliberately, nothing to fix
- 7-8 good, minor issues (slightly soft, imperfect framing)
- 5-6 usable: motion blur, awkward crop, flat light
- 3-4 poor: dark, shaky, subject partly out of frame
- 1-2 barely usable

## content_potential_score (how useful it is for content; independent of quality)
Consider: storytelling, educational value, emotional pull, entertainment, reusability across topics, relevance to Preski (home training, fat loss, physique, discipline), ability to carry a voiceover, fit with a current trend.
- 9-10 distinctive and clearly reusable: clean demonstration of a signature exercise, standout physique/transformation shot, a strong spoken point to camera
- 7-8 solid supporting footage for a core theme
- 5-6 generic b-roll
- 1-4 little reuse value (accidental, repetitive, off-brand)
A shaky phone clip of a PB can score 9 here and 4 visually.

## hook_potential_score
Would the first 2 seconds stop a scroll? Visible effort, unusual kit, clear before/after, bold physique frame, a striking spoken claim. Most clips are 3-6.

## audio_quality_score
Only for clips with audio you can evidence (speech clear vs wind/music/noise). Otherwise null.

## stability_score
Camera steadiness across the 4 contact-sheet frames: tripod-steady 9-10, minor handheld 6-8, visibly shaky 1-5.

## talking_head
1 only when the creator is facing camera and speaking (mouth/pose/framing evidence, or a transcript). Describe expression and engagement in `description`.

## Fitness category cheat-sheet
- Named exercise needs visible evidence (equipment + movement). Otherwise tag the muscle group or activity only.
- Cardio covers walking pad, treadmill, incline, running, cycling, skipping, swimming, boxing/conditioning.
- Physique: shirtless/flexing/mirror/progress shots. Tag `lean` only if it genuinely looks lean.
