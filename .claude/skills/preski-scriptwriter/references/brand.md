# Preski Fitness brand reference

## Creator
Kye, aka King Shredder. Full-time fitness creator and Level 2 Gym Instructor, building Preski Fitness into an online coaching business.

## Positioning
Get in shape and get shredded at home. No gym needed.

## Current phase
Cutting and getting lean. Still trains heavy but always below max, with controlled form.

## Home kit
Walking pad, weighted vest, two 12.5kg dumbbells, captain's chair, skipping ropes, boxing gloves, floor and wall. Films in the garden.

## Brand colours
Navy and red.

## Audience (mostly 18-34)
People who want to get in shape at home:
- socially anxious or introverted
- don't want a gym
- low budget
- prefer training alone with bodyweight
- family commitments, can't leave home for long
- want full control of their training environment

## Voice
British English. Direct, practical, conversational, serious. Plain and simple. One person talking to camera, not a script. No "too human-sounding" polish.

Never: fake motivation, long intros, robotic phrasing, unsupported health claims, guaranteed results, clone videos, AI avatars, cinematic or overproduced edits.

Not every video is a listicle. Not every video is a coaching sell.

## Format
Jump-cut, talk-to-camera, real training footage, simple text overlays. CapCut or Instagram Edits. Talking-head dialogue on every video.

Value standard: worth paying £1,000 for. Specific, actionable, the kind of video people save.

## Benchmarks (top performers)
- Weighted crunches: 68K
- Dumbbell-only: 30K
- Under-25-minutes home workout: 28K

## DM CTA keywords
HOME, SHRED, PROTEIN, ABS. Only when the video has earned it.

## Funnel stages
- Top: reach
- Middle: warm up
- Bottom: convert
