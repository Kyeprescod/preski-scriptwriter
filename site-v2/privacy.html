Privacy

Draft in plain English. Have it checked before you rely on it.

What I collect

Your email address when you request a free guide. When you apply for coaching: your name, email, age, goals, training, health information you choose to share, and your Instagram handle if you add it.

Why

To send you the guide, to read and reply to your application, and to send you training and nutrition emails. You can unsubscribe from any email.

Who handles it

Kit (email), Netlify (website hosting and application form). If I add payments, the payment provider handles your card details; I never see them. I don't sell your data.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data at hello@preskifitness.com.
