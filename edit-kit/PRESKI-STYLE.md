# Preski Fitness edit style (this brief overrides the kit's defaults)

Put this file in the root of the Herk Edit Kit folder. Tell the agent to read it first. The kit's own rule is that the project brief controls pacing, palette and typography, so everything below wins over Nate's house style, `MOTION_PHILOSOPHY.md` and the showreel looks.

## Who it's for
Kye, aka King Shredder, a UK online coach and Level 2 Gym Instructor. Preski Fitness: get in shape and get shredded at home, no gym. Audience: mostly 18-34, shy or introverted people, people on a budget, people who train alone or can't leave home for long. British English everywhere on screen.

## The look: plain, real, serious
- One person talking to camera, real training footage, jump cuts, simple text. Not a cinematic edit, not an ad.
- **Format:** 1080x1920 (9:16), 30-60 seconds, 30 fps. Hook in the first 3 seconds.
- **Colours:** navy `#071A3D`, deep navy `#040E22`, red `#E8323C`, white `#FFFFFF`. Red is for the key word or the answer only. Nothing else in the palette.
- **Fonts:** Big Shoulders Display (bold, caps) for hooks and titles, Figtree for small text. The font files are in the `daily-post-v2/fonts` folder of the preski-scriptwriter repo.
- No emojis on screen except at most one in the hook text.

## Cutting
- Cut dead air and retakes between phrases, never inside a phrase. Keep a natural breath so it sounds like one person talking, not a polished script.
- Hard jump cuts. No whoosh, spin, swirl or glitch transitions. No slow-motion cinema looks.
- Allowed movement: a slow push-in or a small zoom change (about 105 to 112 percent) on a jump cut to keep the eye moving.
- Never speed up the voice. Never add or change what Kye says.
- Pace: a visible change (cut, zoom, text or b-roll) at least every 2 to 3 seconds.

## Text on screen
- **Hook text** at the start: short, first person, CAPS on the key words, white on navy with the key word in red. Maximum 6 words per line.
- **Captions:** 2 to 5 words at a time, bold, large, in a dark box so they are readable over any footage. Keep them clear of the mouth in the first 2 seconds. Never use the % symbol in burned-in captions; write "percent".
- **Text pops** for numbers, sets, reps, tempo and kit (for example "12.5 KG x 2", "3 SECOND LOWER"). Only use numbers that are in the script or on the footage.
- **End card:** one line and a keyword CTA only if the script has one (HOME, SHRED, PROTEIN, ABS). Never invent a keyword.

## B-roll (Kye's real footage only)
- Use real clips of Kye: home training, exercise form, walking pad, weighted vest, captain's chair, skipping, boxing gloves, cooking, walking, talking to camera. Filmed in the garden or at home.
- Gym footage is throwback only and must be framed that way ("where it started"). Never imply he trains in a gym now.
- Every shot moves or has a clear action. No frozen stills as filler. A dated progress photo is allowed as a short proof beat, labelled with its real date.
- Match b-roll to the line being said (the script's "B-roll" column).

## Sound
- Voice first, always clear. Music, if any, is quiet, steady and minimal, kept well under the voice. No loud trending-sound edits: Kye adds trending audio in the app himself.
- Sound effects: soft taps, dry clicks, air. No zaps, lasers, risers or pitch-drop plops.

## Never (hard rules)
- No AI avatars, no clones or lookalikes of Kye, no voice cloning, no AI-generated people.
- No AI-generated, edited, retouched or enhanced physique shots. His body is shown only in real, unedited footage and photos.
- No AI-generated b-roll showing people. No AI-generated or stock gym footage passed off as Kye's.
- No other people's faces (gym bystanders, street shots) unless there is consent. If a clip has them, don't use it.
- No private or intimate content, DMs, screenshots of chats or client photos without written permission.
- No guaranteed results, no timelines promised, no medical claims, no steroid talk. Don't invent stats, testimonials or dates.
- Nothing cinematic or overproduced: no film grain, letterbox bars, lens flares, 3D camera moves or heavy colour grades.
- Don't upload his footage to any outside service without asking Kye first, and say what it costs. Paid services (Kie.ai, ElevenLabs) are off unless he says yes. Local Whisper is the default for transcripts.
- Originals are read-only. Never move, rename or delete source footage. Write every edit to a new file.

## How to use it with the Preski scriptwriter
1. The scriptwriter gives a beat-by-beat script. Each beat has the spoken line, on-screen text, shot, b-roll idea and edit note.
2. Treat those beats as the edit plan. Spoken lines come from the recording. On-screen text, b-roll and edit notes come from the script.
3. If a beat asks for footage that isn't available, say so and list what to film. Don't fill it with generated media.

## Check before export (every time)
- The first 3 seconds make someone want to stay, and the hook text is readable.
- Every number and claim on screen matches the script or the footage.
- Captions are in the dark box, nothing covers the mouth in the first 2 seconds, and there is no % sign.
- Voice is clear, with no mistakes or retakes left in.
- Only Kye's real footage, with no bystander faces.
- Length is 30 to 60 seconds, 1080x1920.
- Tell Kye what was changed and anything you weren't sure about.

## The one prompt to start an edit
"Read PRESKI-STYLE.md first. Then use the short-form-edit skill on <path to clip>, with the script at <path or pasted>. Cut silences and mistakes, add the captions and text from the script, and render a 9:16 reel in the Preski style. Don't use any paid services."
